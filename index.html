US County Health & Socioeconomic Visualization

An interactive exploration of health and socioeconomic patterns

About This Dashboard

This interactive dashboard visualizes health indicators and socioeconomic factors across US counties, allowing you to explore connections between different variables.

Created by: Viraj Kishore Charakanam

Data Sources: US Heart & Stroke Atlas

How to Use This Dashboard

Histogram

Shows the distribution of your selected variable across all counties.

Updates automatically when you select points on the scatterplot.

Scatterplot & Brushing

Reveals relationships between two variables. Drag to draw a selection box around points to filter the histogram and map.

Click anywhere outside the selection to reset.

Choropleth Map

Displays geographic patterns with color intensity showing the selected variable's values across counties.

Hover over counties for detailed information.

Linked Views

All visualizations are connected - changes in one affect the others.

This helps discover patterns across different perspectives of the same data.

Insights & Patterns

The dashboard reveals several key patterns in US county health and socioeconomic data:

  • Income & Health Insurance: Counties with lower median incomes tend to have higher percentages of uninsured residents.
  • Stroke & Poverty: There's a visible correlation between stroke prevalence and poverty rates in many regions.
  • Regional Patterns: The map reveals geographic clusters that share similar health and economic characteristics.

Try selecting different variable combinations and using the brush tool to explore these relationships further.
